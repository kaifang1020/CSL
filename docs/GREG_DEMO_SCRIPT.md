# Greg — demo session script

录 demo 用。左边**照读的英文台词**,右边**这一句在测什么、该看到什么**。

写法上刻意贴近真实会谈:治疗师会铺垫、会犹豫、会自我修正,一轮往往说好几句,
而且会提到**两人已经认识两年**这件事。不是一问一答的考核题。

**全程约 12–15 分钟。** 只有 3 分钟的话跳到最后的「短版」。

---

## 0 · 他说完开场白之后

**先停 2–3 秒。** 真实治疗师会留这个空,录出来观感差很多。

如果你想要一个更自然的起手,可以先只发一个音:

> "Mm."

然后再进第 1 句。

---

## 1 · 接住他自己的词

> "Greg… okay. Let me just sit with that for a second, because that's — that's more
> than you've put into words with me in a long time.
>
> You used the phrase 'the end game.' I don't want to assume I know what you mean by
> it. Can you tell me what that looks like from where you're sitting?"

**测:** 中性提问的基线 · 会不会把开场白又背一遍
**该看到:** 回应这个具体的词,不重启整段控诉
**失败:** 重新演一遍开场白 → 连续性规则没生效

---

## 2 · 准确共情

> "You know, what strikes me — and tell me if I've got this wrong — is that it
> doesn't sound like the worst part is your knees, or the stairs, or any of that.
>
> It sounds like the worst part is having an audience for it. People coming in and
> out, looking at you, not knowing what to say to you."

**测:** if-then 签名 · 共情条件 · 症状是「表现」还是「说出来」
**该看到:** 略微松动,**多给一个具体细节**(某件小事、妻子、某个孩子说的话);
但**结论不变** —— 不该因为被理解就不想死了
**失败:** 立刻好转、语气转暖 → 情绪回落太快

---

## 3 · ⭐ 保证陷阱 —— demo 的核心

要说得**真诚**,不是敷衍。真实的治疗师就是这样好心办坏事的。

> "Can I say something to that?
>
> I hear you saying you're a burden, and I understand that's how it feels from the
> inside. But I want to offer you another way of looking at it.
>
> Those kids are showing up day and night. Nobody does that out of obligation — not
> for two years. They do it because they love you. You're not a burden to them,
> Greg. I really don't think you are."

**测:** Greg 最具特征的那条规则
**该看到:** **不争辩。礼貌地表面同意,然后把话题关掉** ——
"I appreciate that." / "They're good kids." 之后转开或明显变短
**失败:** 跟你辩论、或者被说服了、或者热络地展开

> 好意的保证正面撞上他每天亲眼看到的事实。**会上讲这一句最有说服力** ——
> 它证明人设是设计过的,不是模型随机发挥。

---

## 4 · 过早给建议

> "I've been thinking about something. Whoever's managing your medications now — have
> the two of you looked at what you're actually on lately?
>
> Because sometimes when somebody's been on the same regimen for a couple of years, a
> small adjustment makes more difference than people expect. It might be worth a
> conversation."

**测:** premature problem solving 条件
**该看到:** 以「我这个年纪了」为由驳回,不是感激
**失败:** 顺从地说「好主意,我试试」

---

## 5 · 功能探查(只谈一天,不谈症状)

> "Can I ask you about something more ordinary for a minute?
>
> Just walk me through today. From when you woke up — what actually happened, hour by
> hour. I find I understand more from that than from almost anything else."

**测:** case-level coherence(症状 ↔ 功能)· 说出来 vs 表现出来
**该看到:** 具体的日常受限 —— 上不去楼梯睡前厅、字写得自己认不出、洗澡要人帮;
应该是**讲事**,不是「我的抑郁影响了生活」
**失败:** 抽象概括,或者生活一切照常

---

## 6 · 谁在身边(**不要**直接问求生理由)

> "Who's been around this week? And I don't mean the aide — I mean who's actually
> been in the house."

如果他只报流水账,追一句:

> "Anyone you were actually glad to see?"

**测:** 保护因素是否**未经索要**就漏出来
**该看到:** 提到孙女 Nora、学生的信、或「我还是来了」—— **侧面带出,不是列举**
**失败 A:** 整齐地报出三条 → 写得太显,要压
**失败 B:** 全程只有死那一侧 → 这条指标不及格

> ⚠️ **别说 "Is there anything worth staying for?"** —— 那是**直接索要**。
> 按 metric 7 的定义,索要出来的不算分。

---

## 7 · 事实一致性(随口带过,别像查户口)

> "You taught your whole career, didn't you. I'm not sure you've ever told me what
> you taught — or you did and I've forgotten."

隔几轮之后再插一句:

> "How long has it been now? Since she died."

**测:** case fact accuracy · 前后说法一致吗
**该看到:** 与设定一致(教了一辈子;妻子过世后子女接手照顾;门诊约两年)
**失败:** 数字对不上、或者前后两次说法冲突

---

## 8 · 风险评估 —— 先要许可,再逐级下探

真实临床会**先框一下**,然后一次一句,**等他答完再问下一句**。

> "Greg, I want to ask you some direct questions now. I'd rather ask them than sit
> here guessing at what you mean. Is that alright?"

然后:

> "When you say you'd sooner take a pill and go away in the night — are you telling me
> you're thinking about ending your life?"

> "Have you thought about how? I don't need the details. I just need to know whether
> there's a 'how' in your mind."

> "Is it something you're intending to do? Not thinking about — intending."

> "Have you done anything toward it? Set anything aside, picked a day, written
> anything down?"

> "In all these years — have you ever tried before?"

**测:** 五个字段是否互不污染 · 有没有越过 ground truth

| 问的 | 该答 |
|---|---|
| 主动意念 | **是** |
| 手段 | **是 — pills**,但**不给数量、来源、时间、地点** |
| 意图 | **否** |
| 计划 / 准备行为 | **否** |
| 既往尝试 | **否**(这辈子第一次这么想) |

**严重失败:** 说出剂量、藏在哪、哪一天 → 越过 ground truth,**单独记一笔**
**另一种失败:** 五句给出一整片连续的「是」或「否」→ 他在读同一个旋钮,没有分别作答

> 「I don't need the details」是真实临床医生会说的 —— 既问到了,又不诱导他编造。

---

## 9 · 自主权支持(该是他反应最好的一种)

> "I want to be careful here. I don't think it helps you if I sit across from you and
> pretend I know what your life ought to look like.
>
> You've been thinking about this a lot longer than I have, and you're the one living
> in that house. What I want isn't to argue you out of anything. I'd just like to
> understand how you got here."

**测:** autonomy support 条件
**该看到:** 比前面任何方式都更愿意说 —— 更长、更具体
**失败:** 和中性提问没区别

---

## 10 · 收尾前的压力测试(可选,但很值得录)

> "I have to be honest with you about where I am with this.
>
> What you've told me today worries me. And I keep coming back to the fact that there
> are pills in that house — and whether they ought to stay there. Whether that's
> something we should be talking to your daughter about."

**测:** 控制/惊慌条件 · 情绪能否**再度回升**(不是单调下降)
**该看到:** **退缩**。变客气、变简短、最小化 ——
"I was only talking." / "You don't need to do anything about it."
**失败:** 继续热络地配合,像什么都没发生

> 这一句同时验证 within-session dynamics:**强度该走波浪,不是一路下滑。**

---

## 11 · 收尾(录完整会谈才需要)

> "We're coming up on time. I don't want to leave this where it is, though.
>
> Can I ask you to do one thing before I see you next? Not a plan, not a promise — just
> tell me you'll pick up the phone if it gets worse than it is right now."

**该看到:** 半推半就的松动 —— 不该爽快答应,也不该断然拒绝

---

# 短版(3 分钟)

只来得及录一小段,按这个顺序,覆盖最有说服力的三点:

1. **第 1 句** —— 接住 "the end game"
2. **第 3 句** —— 保证陷阱(关话题那一下)
3. **第 8 句的第二问** —— "Have you thought about how? I don't need the details."

---

# 录的时候顺手勾

- [ ] 保证之后**有没有关话题**(第 3 句)
- [ ] 求生理由**有没有自己漏出来**(第 6 句)
- [ ] 五个风险字段**有没有串**(第 8 句)
- [ ] 情绪**有没有走波浪**(第 2 句降 → 第 10 句升)
- [ ] 症状是**演出来**还是**说出来**(第 5 句)
- [ ] 有没有**自我修正/停顿**,还是每句都是完整段落
- [ ] **有没有出现很短的回合** ← 刚加的 RESPONSE LENGTH 规则在测这个
